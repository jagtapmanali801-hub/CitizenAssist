# Citizen Assist — Official Portal Link Audit

This file records the portal destination assigned to each service in `data/services.js`.

Important:
- These are official government/department starting points for the service category.
- Where the government provides a specific application route, the mapping uses it.
- Where the service label is broad or the correct authority depends on the user's municipality, district, exam, or case, the link is an official department portal rather than a guaranteed direct form.
- Citizen Assist does not submit applications or retrieve live application status.
- Government URLs can change; recheck them periodically.
