# Citizen Assist – Government Digital Portal Assistance Website

**Making Government Digital Services Easier for Everyone**

## About the Project

Citizen Assist is a web-based academic project developed to help citizens understand and access various government digital services and online portals.

The main purpose of this website is to make digital government services easier to understand, especially for people who face difficulties while using online government platforms.

This project was developed as part of a Semester 5 team-based academic project.

## Project Objectives

- Help citizens understand government digital portals.
- Provide information about important government services.
- Make online services easier to access and navigate.
- Improve digital awareness among citizens.
- Promote accessible and user-friendly digital experiences.

## Features

- User-friendly website interface.
- Information about government digital services.
- Guidance for accessing important government portals.
- Accessibility-focused interface.
- Language accessibility support.
- Chatbot assistance feature.
- Simple and easy-to-understand navigation.
